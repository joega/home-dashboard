By default, daytime uses Android’s manual/adaptive brightness. Uncheck the daytime option to use a fixed level. When scheduling is off, Android brightness takes over at all times. The panel has a hardware minimum, so very low percentages may look the same. Android settings remain adjustable outside the kiosk.

Inactivity

Touch restores the scheduled brightness. Idle dimming never makes the screen brighter. Reading without touching counts as inactivity; this is off by default.

Motion wake and screen-off: not available yet

This frame currently exposes light, acceleration and temperature sensors, with no verified presence signal. The bezel sensor is being investigated. Full screen-off stays disabled until a reliable motion wake path is confirmed. Idle dimming keeps the display and touchscreen active.
